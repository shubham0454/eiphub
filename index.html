About the Hub
Explore · Innovate · Participate

Each of the four days follows a distinct theme guiding attendees through Ethereum standards, from EIP governance on day one to enterprise adoption on day four.

Day 1

EIP Process and Governance

  • Intro to EIPs and lifecycle
  • GitHub workflows
  • Write your first EIP
  • Contributor onboarding
  • EIP Office Hours
  • Meet the Editors
Day 2

Protocol and Core Ethereum

  • Ethereum roadmap
  • Meta EIPs and upgrades
  • Glamsterdam and Hegotá
  • Client implementation
  • Testing and interop
  • Protocol tooling
Day 3

Application Standards and ERCs

  • ERC-7935, ERC-8004 and more
  • Chain ID and wallet standards
  • Reference implementations
  • Smart contract security
  • ERC review clinics
  • Builder networking
Day 4

Enterprise Ethereum

  • Standards adoption
  • Real world implementations
  • Enterprise architecture
  • Compliance and governance
  • EEA collaboration
  • Enterprise roundtables

A collaborative space at Devcon 8 India dedicated to Ethereum Improvement Proposals, ERC standards, protocol upgrades, and standards adoption. It brings together contributors from across the Ethereum ecosystem for technical discussions, office hours, and community engagement.
Anyone interested in shaping Ethereum: first-time EIP authors, protocol researchers, client developers, ERC authors, wallet developers, infrastructure teams, enterprise architects, educators, students, and open-source maintainers. Special focus on contributors from India and the wider APAC region.
Inside the Devcon 8 India venue. A Devcon ticket is required to access the space. Register on Luma to receive location details and updates as they are confirmed.
The full schedule is on Luma at lu.ma/czi58jpe. Register there and you will get session reminders and agenda updates directly in your calendar.
Selected sessions will be livestreamed and recorded. If you cannot join in person, you can watch recordings afterward.
